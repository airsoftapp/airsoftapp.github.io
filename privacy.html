Privacy Policy

Effective: 12 November 2026. If this English version and the Turkish version differ, the Turkish version prevails.

This policy explains which personal data the Airsoft App mobile app ("the App") processes, why, who it is shared with and how you can use your rights. It serves as the information notice under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data controller

AirsoftApptr (individual developer)
Email: airsoftapptr@gmail.com

2. What we collect

About location

  • Location is used only when you turn sharing on in the Map tab. It is off by default.
  • While sharing is on, location is also collected in the background so it keeps working with the phone in your pocket (on Android a "sharing your location" notification is shown).
  • The server keeps only your latest position, not a history. When you turn sharing off, the latest position is deleted from the server too.
  • Only your teammates see your location; during a live match only players on your side do. Other sides' positions are never sent to you, nor yours to them.

Data that stays on your phone

Your inventory, gear lists, field and match notes, checklists, FPS records, blocked users, radio messages, and bug and message reports are currently stored only on your phone and are not sent to the server. They are removed from the phone when you sign out.

What we don't collect

The App contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Legal bases

  • Performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)): account, profile, team and match data — we cannot provide the App without them.
  • Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), KVKK Art. 5(1)): location sharing. You can withdraw it at any time by turning sharing off on the Map or removing the location permission in your phone's settings.
  • Legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f), KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)): technical logs, security and abuse prevention.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, team and match data: until your account is deleted.
  • Live location: only the latest position while sharing is on; deleted when you turn it off.
  • Map pins: until the person who placed them deletes them or the team/match is deleted.
  • Technical logs: for the providers' log retention periods (typically days).

6. Deleting your account and data

To delete your account and all of your data on the server, email airsoftapptr@gmail.com from your registered address with the subject "Account deletion request". We will do so within 30 days and confirm. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, team memberships, match records, location and map pins.

Signing out removes the data on your phone; it does not delete your account on the server.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR and KVKK Art. 11 you can ask whether your data is processed, access it, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent. Send requests to airsoftapptr@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or, in the EU, to your country's data protection authority.

8. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Every table on the server is protected by row-level security rules so that players can reach only the data they are allowed to see.

9. Children

The App is not directed at anyone under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data. Fields may have their own age limits; check a field's rules before you go.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will announce significant changes in the App or by email; the current version is always on this page.
