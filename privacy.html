HarpZone is for users aged 18 and older. You confirm that you are 18 or older when you create your profile. We do not provide the service to, or knowingly collect data from, anyone under 18. Fields may have their own age rules; check them before you go.

About location

Location is used only when you turn on sharing on the Map tab; it is off by default. While sharing is on, location is also collected in the background so it keeps working with the phone in your pocket (on Android a persistent notification shows). Only your latest position is kept on the server, never a history, and it is deleted when you turn sharing off.

Who sees what

  • Your callsign, country, role and camouflage are visible to the players in your team and in the matches you join.
  • Your player card (callsign, country, role, experience level, camouflage, your team's name and city, the guns in your inventory, their attachments, your gun photos and the marks on them) is visible to every signed-in player; tapping your name in Radio opens it. Gun photos are stored at a public address anyone with the link can open; we recommend not showing your face or personal details in them.
  • Your teammates see your location and map markers; during a live match only players on your side do. Other sides' positions are never sent to you, and yours are never sent to them.
  • Your Radio messages show with your callsign and role: in the country channel to everyone who opens it, in the team channel to your team, in the match channel to your side only.
  • Your Marketplace listings (including photos and your phone number) are visible, with your callsign, to every signed-in player. Listing photos are stored at a public address anyone with the link can open. Only you add your phone number to a listing.

Data that stays on your phone

Your gear lists, field and match notes, checklists, FPS logs are stored only on your phone and never sent to the server. They are deleted from the phone when you sign out.

Advertising and tracking

The Marketplace has an ad space labelled "SPONSORLU" (sponsored); the developer adds these ads and everyone sees the same ones. The App has no personalised ads, no third-party ad networks, no usage analytics and no tracking tools. We do not sell your data.

4. Legal bases

  • Performance of a contract (KVKK art. 5/2-c, GDPR art. 6/1-b): account, profile, team, match, Radio, Marketplace and inventory data — we cannot provide the App without them.
  • Consent (KVKK art. 5/1, GDPR art. 6/1-a): location sharing and notifications. You can withdraw it at any time in the App (the sharing switch on the Map, match notifications in Settings) or in your phone's settings.
  • Legitimate interests (KVKK art. 5/2-f, GDPR art. 6/1-f): technical logs, security, preventing fraud and abuse.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account, profile, team and match data: until your account is deleted.
  • Live location: only the latest position while sharing is on; deleted when you turn sharing off.
  • Map markers: until the person who placed them deletes them or the team/match is deleted.
  • Radio messages: 30 days, then deleted automatically. Reports are deleted with the message.
  • Gun photo reports: until the team decides (removes the photo or dismisses the report) or the photo is deleted.
  • Blocked players: until you unblock them or your account is deleted.
  • Marketplace listings and photos: listings run for 60 days; an expired listing is hidden from others but stays in your listings until you delete it or your account is deleted.
  • Inventory and gun photos: until you remove the gun from your inventory, remove the photo or your account is deleted.
  • Support requests: until your account is deleted; the notification emails sent to the team are deleted within 1 year after the request is resolved.
  • Push token: until you sign out, turn notifications off or your account is deleted.
  • Technical logs: for the providers' log retention periods (usually a few days).

7. Deleting your account and data

In the App: Profile → Account → Hesabımı sil (Delete my account). Once you confirm, your account and all your data on the server (including Marketplace and gun photos) are deleted immediately and permanently. If you are a team captain, captaincy passes to the team's oldest member; matches you opened are deleted.

If you cannot use the App: email airsoftapptr@gmail.com from your registered address with the subject "Account deletion request". We complete it within 30 days and let you know.

8. Your rights

Under KVKK art. 11 and the GDPR you can learn whether your data is processed, access it, have it corrected or deleted, restrict processing, object, receive it in a portable form and withdraw consent. Send requests to airsoftapptr@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board or, in the EU, to your country's data protection authority.

9. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Every table on the server is protected by row-level security rules so players can reach only the data they are allowed to see.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We announce significant changes in the App or by email; the current version is always on this page.
